# Vertical DNA: cinematic / hyperreal short (story, commercial, trailer)

It should look like it was shot by a real crew with a real camera. **The image and its emotion come first, and every shot earns its place.**

## What makes it work

- **The hook image:**
  - Open on the single most striking frame of the piece, plus an open question.
  - Each beat connects to the next with "but" or "therefore", never "and then".
- **Shot grammar:**
  - Motivated camera moves: slow push for realisation, handheld for chaos, locked for tension.
  - Each shot has one action and one reveal. Use 2–4 s shots, and longer only to hold emotion.
- **Light:**
  - One motivated key with a practical in frame and a defined colour temperature (e.g. 3200K tungsten against 5600K window).
  - The palette is locked as hex values in the style sheet.
- **Realism:** natural skin texture, imperfect props, real lens behaviour (shallow DOF, flare), and the "film" finish (grain, highlight roll-off).
- **Sound:**
  - Design the room tone first, then foley, then music.
  - Music swells only after the turn. Silence is a tool here, unlike in sitcoms.

## Structure

- **Commercial (15–30 s):** hook image → problem → reveal → product in use → end card.
- **Story (30–90 s):** a compressed Story Circle (want → go → find → pay → return changed).

## Production settings

- **Video:** 720p is required (no SD finish hides detail). Consider Seedance 2 Fast or Kling 3.0 for silent beats and Wan for dialogue.
- **Cheap first pass:** a 5 s hook-shot test.

## QC

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Hook frame alone would stop a scroll | yes |
| Camera move matches the beat's emotion | yes |
| Palette and light hold across cuts | yes |
| No AI sheen | yes |
