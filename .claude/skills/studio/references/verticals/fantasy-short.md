# Vertical DNA: fantasy short (epic or cozy, 30–90 s)

Dragons, enchanted forests, floating cities, spirits, magic duels, cozy wizard cottages. **One unforgettable image and one wonder moment** sell the piece; the story is simple so the world can be big.

## What makes it work

- **The world is the hook.** The first frame is the most spectacular image of the piece: scale, light and something impossible. Viewers stop scrolling for the image, then stay for the question it raises.
- **One rule of magic, shown, not explained.** "Lanterns wake the sleeping giants." Show it working once early and once at the climax.
- **Scale contrast:** a tiny figure against a vast thing (a dragon, a tree, a castle in the clouds). Wide shots carry the genre; use them for reveals.
- **Light tells the mood:** golden hour and god rays for wonder, moonlight and blue fog for mystery, firelight for danger, warm interiors for cozy fantasy.
- **Sound sells the magic:** choir or strings swells, a shimmer on every spell, deep low-end for creatures, and silence right before the reveal.
- **Two sub-genres, two paces:**
  - *Epic:* threat → journey → confrontation → awe. Faster cuts, rising music.
  - *Cozy:* small want → gentle magic → warm payoff. Slow, held shots, soft score. Pairs well with long-form ambient (see `meditation-longform.md`).

## Structure (30–90 s)

1. The impossible image (0–3 s), with a question.
2. The character and their want (one line or one action).
3. The magic rule shown.
4. Escalation: the world pushes back.
5. The wonder or confrontation moment, the biggest shot.
6. A button: a quiet final image that hints the world goes on (sets up a series).

## Writing rules

- Narration, if used, is sparse and mythic: "In the year the moon fell asleep..." Dialogue stays minimal; the image carries it.
- Invent original names, creatures and places. Never use existing franchises, creatures or characters that belong to someone else.
- Keep proper nouns to 2–3 per short so viewers aren't lost.

## Production settings

- **Style:** pick one and lock it in a style sheet: painterly (oil, gouache), anime fantasy, or photoreal cinematic. Never mix them.
- **Stills:** GPT Image 2.5 at 2K for the hero frames; the hero image doubles as the thumbnail.
- **Video:** Seedance 2 family at 720p (9:16 for Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube). Creature and magic shots benefit from first-frame anchoring on an approved still, plus a last frame when the move must land on a composition.
- **Music:** Suno, orchestral or choir for epic, harp, flute and soft strings for cozy. **Narration:** ElevenLabs, a deep storyteller voice for epic, warm and soft for cozy.
- **Edit:** the sitcom editor's laughs off; music stings on reveals; 0.5–1 s held frames on wonder shots; the cinematic finish.

## QC

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| First frame is the strongest image | yes |
| The magic rule is shown at least twice | yes |
| Style holds across shots (no drift from painterly to photoreal) | yes |
| Creature and character designs stay consistent | yes |
| No recognizable franchise elements | yes |
| Music swells land on the reveal (±0.25 s) | yes |
