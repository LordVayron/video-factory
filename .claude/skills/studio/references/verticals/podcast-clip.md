# Vertical DNA: podcast clip (two people at mics)

One complete idea, with the strongest line first. **The exchange between the two people is the content.**

## What makes it work

- **Hook, context, payoff.**
  - Open on the strongest statement, question or tension, and cut the first half-sentence.
  - Use the shortest complete argument, not the shortest quote.
  - End right after the payoff, with no "so, yeah". ([Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/blog/how-to-turn-podcast-into-shorts-reels-tiktoks-2026))
- **Never write a hook the clip can't back up.**
- **Framing:**
  - Crop head and chest, with the eyes on the upper third and room for captions.
  - Cut to whoever speaks, or stack both people when the reaction matters.
  - Keep one layout per clip. ([quso](https://quso.ai/blog/how-to-edit-podcast-shorts-like-a-pro.html))
- **Captions are the delivery mechanism.** Use 2 lines max with word highlight, and check names and numbers by hand.
- **Audio:** natural rhythm, no over-processing. Remove dead air but keep breaths that carry emotion.

## Look

Warm studio, two mics on arms, practical lights, a shelf or neon in the background, and a static two-shot plus two singles. Use the podcast style sheet.

## Writing

- **Banter has a real disagreement or reveal.** The host plays the straight man; the guest drops the surprising claim.
- **Lines run 8–20 words**, with interruptions and laughs allowed.

## Pacing QC

| Metric | Pass |
|---|---|
| Claim within 3 s | yes |
| Gaps | ≤ 0.4 s |
| Ends on payoff | yes |
| Speaker on screen = speaker talking | yes |
