# Vertical DNA: anime short (comedy or action)

Strong poses, hard timing and big contrast. Most frames hold still and one moment explodes. **The timing of the reaction is the joke.**

## What makes it work

- **Limited animation is a style, not a flaw.**
  - Most of the time is held poses, mouth-only movement, cycles and reused backgrounds.
  - Spend motion on the key beat. ([Limited animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_animation))
- **Timing contrast:**
  - A slow anticipation makes a fast action read as fast.
  - Hold the important pose long enough to read, and use moving holds instead of dead stops. ([pixune](https://pixune.com/blog/timing-in-animation/))
- **Use only one effect per moment:** a smear, a speed-line burst or a 1–2 frame impact frame, never all three. ([Prolific](https://prolificstudio.co/blog/smear-frames/))
- **Comedy grammar:**
  - Setup → escalation → a held beat on the face as it processes → an exaggerated reaction face (chibi, sweat drop, blank eyes) with a sound cue on the exact frame.
  - The reaction face is the punchline.
- **Sound design:** whooshes, impact hits, the comedic "boing", and a dramatic sting on reveals. The voice acting is exaggerated.

## Structure (20–40 s)

Hook pose → setup line → escalation ×2 → impact/reaction beat → tag line on a still frame.

## Production settings

- **Style:** anime style sheet (cel shading, clean line, flat colour fields, painted backgrounds).
- **Video:** the cheapest model that holds line style. Test Seedance 2 Mini and Grok Imagine against the style north star.
- **Resolution:** 480p with no finish is acceptable for a cel look; an upscale only if lines shimmer.

## QC

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Line style holds across cuts | yes |
| No 3D-looking shading drift | yes |
| Reaction frame lands on the sound cue (±2 frames) | yes |
| Max one effect per beat | yes |
