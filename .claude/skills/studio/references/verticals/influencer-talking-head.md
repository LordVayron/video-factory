# Vertical DNA: AI influencer / "clone me" talking to camera

It should feel like a friend talking to you, one-on-one, with no performance voice. **What matters most is the opinion or story, said in the first line.**

## What makes it work

- **The first sentence is the thesis or the hook.** Strip the "hey guys".
- **Something changes on screen every 2–4 s.** That can be a jump cut, a reframe (punch-in to a close-up), a text pop, B-roll, or a prop. A stretch longer than about 1 s of silence with no visual cue loses viewers. ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/advanced-retention-editing-cutting-patterns-that-keep-viewers-past-minute-8), [socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/retention-techniques-keep-viewers-watching-2026))
- **Jump cuts remove every um and pause.** They are the genre's grammar. Cover hard jumps with a punch-in so they read as intentional. ([mysocial](https://mysocial.io/blog/youtube-presenter-led-videos))
- **Pace:** 140–170 wpm, natural breaths. A 1–2 s pause is allowed only before the key line.
- **Captions:** word-by-word, 1–2 lines, above the UI safe zone.
- **Realism:** eye-line to the lens, hand gestures, small imperfections, a real room, window light. Hyperreal style, "film" finish.

## Structure (30–60 s)

1. Hook line
2. Why you should care
3. Three points or one story with a twist
4. Payoff line
5. Soft CTA or open loop

## Production settings

- **Identity:** 5-image identity set (sheet, face close-up, full body, profiles, detail grid) plus a 6 s clean voice sample.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs. For "clone me", only the user's own likeness and voice, with their consent.
- **Long monologues:** Kling Avatar / InfiniteTalk lip-sync on the voice track (cheapest per second). Seedance or Wan for B-roll and gestures.
- **Clips over 15 s:** chain from the last frame.
- **Resolution:** 720p.

## Pacing QC

| Metric | Pass |
|---|---|
| Thesis said within 3 s | yes |
| Longest static stretch with no visual change | ≤ 4 s |
| Silence | ≤ 0.5 s |
| wpm | 140–170 |
| Lip sync visibly matches | yes |
